Personal data protection

Share less. Check more.

Personal data includes details that identify you, describe your life, or help someone reach you.

☎

Contact and home details

Keep phone numbers, email addresses, and home addresses private where possible. Avoid posting them publicly.

▤

Identity documents and photos

Only share ID images with a verified, necessary service. Be careful about photos that reveal badges, documents, or other people's details.

⌖

Location and social accounts

Limit location access and avoid sharing live location or routines with a broad audience. Review social account visibility regularly.

Five practical checks

  1. App permissions: Allow camera, microphone, contacts, or location only when needed.
  2. Privacy settings: Check who can see your profile, posts, and contact details.
  3. Public Wi-Fi: Avoid sensitive tasks on networks you do not trust; use your mobile connection when possible.
  4. Suspicious links: Check the sender and website address. Do not sign in from an unexpected message.
  5. Data requests: Ask whether the information is necessary and how it will be used before sharing.
